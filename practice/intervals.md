# Intervals — interview set

Extra practice for this topic, on top of the problems in `notes/intervals.md` and the
A2Z sheet. `tools/build_site.py` reads this file into the Problems tab.

- **Level** — `basic` · `core` · `pro`. Defaults to LeetCode's Easy · Medium · Hard,
  raised by hand where a problem plays harder than its label.
- **Companies** — indicative only. They reflect problems commonly reported for
  Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Netflix, Salesforce, Spotify and Sprinklr interviews,
  not LeetCode's premium company data. Treat a tag as "reported", not "verified".
- A row whose problem this topic already lists (from your notes or A2Z) is not added
  twice: it lends its company tags and level to the existing row.
- 🔒 marks a LeetCode Premium problem.
